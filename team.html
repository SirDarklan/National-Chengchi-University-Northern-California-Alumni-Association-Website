Lily Wang
王莉莉

B.A. in Journalism, NCCU

Lily Wang graduated in 1972. After completing an internship during her junior year, she was retained by the Economic Daily. As a result, she began her career as a reporter for the Industrial and Commercial Service Department during her senior year. At the time, the Economic Daily was in its fourth year of publication.

During her eight years working at the Economic Daily, she gained a wealth of valuable experience. She interacted with numerous business and corporate professionals and visited many factories to observe their production management. This proved to be incredibly helpful for her future career development.

Later, she transitioned to working in foreign trade import-export companies and the foreign trade departments of manufacturing plants. Due to work requirements, she was dispatched to a branch office in Los Angeles in 1987, which marked her entry into the American corporate world.

In 1999, she joined Apple, becoming a Global Supply Manager. During her ten years at Apple, she served as the President of the Taipei First Girls High School Northern California Alumni Association, as well as the President of the National Chengchi University Alumni Association of Northern California.

After retiring in 2019, she moved to Phoenix, Arizona, where she currently resides. She is deeply honored to have the opportunity to serve the NCCU Northern California Foundation once again.
