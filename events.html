Activities, Events, and Programs

Educational Programs and Public Forums

Our foundation organizes and sponsors educational programs open to the general public.
These include public lectures on topics including international affairs, economics, public policy, science and technology, and Taiwan-U.S. relations, featuring speakers from academia, government, and industry.
We also provide professional development workshops and networking forums that support career development, entrepreneurship, and civic engagement among community members.
Finally, we promote educational outreach activities aimed at youth in the Northern California community, including mentorship programs, college preparation workshops, and STEM education initiatives.

Cultural Exchange and Heritage Programs

We also organize and sponsor cultural programs that promote understanding of Taiwanese and Chinese culture and foster cross-cultural with the community of Northern California.
We hold annual and periodic public events celebrating Taiwanese and Chinese cultural heritage, including cultural festivals, art exhibitions, traditional performing arts, and holiday observances, all of which are open to and designed for the general public.
Furthermore, we also promote cultural education programs introducing Taiwanese and Chinese history, language arts, and traditions to students and the broader community through school outreach partnerships.
We actively seek partnerships with other cultural organizations, universities, and community groups to co-present educational and cultural programs that serve diverse audiences.

Scholarship and Educational Assistance

Additionally, our foundation also provides merit-based and need-based scholarships to provide educational assistance to students.
Annual scholarship awards are given to undergraduate and graduate students of demonstrated academic merit and/or financial need, with preference given to students with ties to NCCU or the Northern California community. Scholarships are open to qualified students regardless of race, color, religion, national orgin, sex, disability, or age.
These scholarships are awarded through an open, publicly advertised application process reviewed by the Foundation's Scholarship Committee. Selection criteria, amounts, and deadlines will be posted here and publicly announced.
Our Foundation also provides grants to support students or educators attending conferences, pursuing research, or engaged in educational activities that align with the Foundation's mission.
